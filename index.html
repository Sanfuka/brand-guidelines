Brand

One designer, twenty years of systems, built to stretch from a business card to a building.

Milan Design is the practice of Alessandro Milan. The identity borrows from the print shop: ink, uncoated paper, and the yellow of a proofing pen. The type stretches and compresses, the same way a good identity system has to adapt without losing its shape.

Principles

Built on a gridEvery element sits on the 12-column grid. Press G to see it on this page.
One family, many widthsArchivo carries everything. Contrast comes from width and weight, never from a second typeface.
Yellow is a mark, not a fillProof yellow points at something. Use it for one thing per layout.

Voice

Plain words, short sentences, no selling.

Write the way you would explain the work across a table. Say what was made and why it works. Leave the adjectives out.

Instead ofWe craft bold, innovative brand experiences that truly resonate.
WriteI design identity systems that work across print, packaging and space.
Instead ofLet’s embark on a creative journey together!
WriteSend me the brief. I’ll reply within two working days.
